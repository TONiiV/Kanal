# Kanal design context

For UI and brand changes, use [Conversation K](docs/design/conversation-k.md). This is the
current visual specification and supersedes the historical monochrome-only, square-corner design.
Use [meeting workspace](docs/design/meeting-workspace.md) for the approved three-column layout,
navigation and interaction requirements. Historical prototypes are layout references.

The operator works during technical Chinese/German/Polish meetings; participants glance at a
read-only translated transcript on their phones. Part numbers, tolerances, dates and speaker
identity must remain legible. The product should feel precise, approachable and calm.

Keep system-font fallbacks for Latin diacritics and CJK. Desktop brushes are explicitly light;
mobile supports both colour schemes. Preserve the one-control mobile reading flow, up to four
desktop language columns and the recording-consent and pause boundaries.
