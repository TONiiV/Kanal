## Design Context

For desktop UI work, read [Current UI design](docs/design/ui-design.md), confirmed on 2026-10-05.
It overrides conflicting visual and layout details in earlier design documents and prototypes. Use
[meeting workspace](docs/design/meeting-workspace.md) for unchanged interaction requirements and
[Conversation K](docs/design/conversation-k.md) for brand asset generation and mobile rules. The
approved [preview](docs/design/swiss-review/index.html) illustrates the target, not shipped
functionality. [docs/design/README.md](docs/design/README.md) indexes all design documents.

The operator works during technical Chinese/German/Polish meetings; participants glance at a
read-only translated transcript on their phones. Part numbers, tolerances, dates and speaker
identity must remain legible. The product should feel precise, approachable and calm.

Keep transcription content readable in Chinese, German and Polish. Brand controls, speaker
identity and recording state use distinct semantic resources. Mobile follows system light/dark
preferences. Preserve the one-control mobile reading flow, and all existing consent, capture,
pause, language-limit and meeting-record behaviour.

The hard constraints in [`CLAUDE.md`](CLAUDE.md#hard-constraints) — fonts, font-stack order, offline
cache, PRD-frozen layout, Avalonia limits, app-owned brushes and colours per variant — apply to every UI change.
